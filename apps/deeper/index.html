Deeper

A puzzle roguelike for the Game Boy Advance, spin-off of Dwarves Manager: dig your way down through a branching map of logic puzzles, monsters, walls and merchants, from the surface to the core. Four languages, playable right here thanks to EmulatorJS.

Controls

Arrow keysD-pad — move the cursor, choose the next room on the map
ZA — dig, place ore or a block, extend the gallery, buy, descend; hammer it at a wall
XB — mark a cell, erase, back up, next block; leave the counter
QL — use a hint token
ER — turn the block in a cavity
VSELECT — the rules of the room and its controls
EnterSTART — pause menu (resume, give up for a life, save and quit)

Hold A or B while moving to act on every cell the cursor crosses. Keys can be remapped, and gamepads are supported, from the settings menu at the bottom right of the screen. Your run, gear and logbook are saved in the browser (battery save).
